# Combined framework: Working Genius × Amazon Leadership Principles

The six Working Genius types (Wonder, Invention, Discernment, Galvanizing,
Enablement, Tenacity) describe the kinds of work that give a person energy.
Amazon's 16 Leadership Principles describe behaviors a leader is expected to
show. This framework uses the six geniuses as the structure and the
principles as the observable behaviors inside each one.

Each principle has one primary home so that scores roll up cleanly. Where a
principle clearly spans two geniuses, the second is listed as a secondary.

## The map

| Phase | Genius | What it does | Primary principles | Secondary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ideation | **Wonder** | Asks whether things could be better; spots the unmet need | Customer Obsession · Learn and Be Curious · Think Big | Success and Scale Bring Broad Responsibility |
| Ideation | **Invention** | Creates the novel solution | Invent and Simplify · Frugality | Think Big |
| Activation | **Discernment** | Judges whether the idea is right | Are Right, A Lot · Dive Deep · Success and Scale Bring Broad Responsibility | Insist on the Highest Standards · Customer Obsession |
| Activation | **Galvanizing** | Rallies people to act | Bias for Action · Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit | Think Big |
| Implementation | **Enablement** | Gives people the support they need | Earn Trust · Hire and Develop the Best · Strive to be Earth's Best Employer | — |
| Implementation | **Tenacity** | Pushes the work over the line | Deliver Results · Ownership · Insist on the Highest Standards | Bias for Action |

## Why each principle sits where it does

**Wonder**
- *Customer Obsession*: starts from the customer and works backwards, which is the question "what do they need that they don't have?"
- *Learn and Be Curious*: the closest match in the set; curiosity about possibilities.
- *Think Big*: seeing a larger potential than others see. The "communicate a bold direction" half belongs to Galvanizing.

**Invention**
- *Invent and Simplify*: the direct match. Simplifying is itself an inventive act.
- *Frugality*: Amazon defines it as constraints that "breed resourcefulness, self-sufficiency, and invention", so it is scored here as inventing under constraint, not as cost control.

**Discernment**
- *Are Right, A Lot*: good judgment and instincts, plus seeking out views that disconfirm your own.
- *Dive Deep*: being skeptical when the metrics and the anecdotes disagree.
- *Success and Scale Bring Broad Responsibility*: weighing the second-order effects of a decision.

**Galvanizing**
- *Bias for Action*: getting people moving on reversible decisions without waiting for certainty.
- *Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit*: challenging openly, then rallying fully behind the decision.

**Enablement**
- *Earn Trust*: listening, candor, and respect, which are what make help welcome.
- *Hire and Develop the Best*: coaching and raising the bar for others.
- *Strive to be Earth's Best Employer*: building an environment where others can succeed.

**Tenacity**
- *Deliver Results*: the direct match; finishing despite setbacks.
- *Ownership*: never "that's not my job"; following through over the long term.
- *Insist on the Highest Standards*: defects don't get sent down the line. The "recognizing what good looks like" half belongs to Discernment.

## How the two fit together in an assessment

The two models measure different things, and the combination is useful
because of that difference:

- **Working Genius** asks what gives you energy. People typically have two
  geniuses, two competencies, and two frustrations.
- **Leadership Principles** ask what you actually do. A leader is expected to
  show all 16.

So each genius gets two readings for a founder:

1. **Energy**: how much they are drawn to that kind of work.
2. **Behavior**: evidence that they practice the principles under it.

| | High behavior | Low behavior |
|---|---|---|
| **High energy** | Strength: lean on it | Untapped: coachable quickly |
| **Low energy** | Burnout risk: doing it well at a personal cost | Gap: cover with a co-founder or an early hire |

A founding team should cover all six geniuses between its members. The
bottom row of the table is where the assessment says something a single
model would miss.

## Open choices

- Think Big, Insist on the Highest Standards, and Customer Obsession each
  have a defensible second home. If single-homing distorts the scores, split
  their weight 50/50 across the two geniuses.
- Galvanizing and Invention have two primary principles each and the others
  have three, so genius scores should be averaged, not summed.
