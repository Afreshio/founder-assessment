### Stress and recovery toolkit (used in week 2 and throughout)

- **4-7-8 breathing** (popularized by Dr. Andrew Weil): breathe out fully; in through the nose for 4; hold for 7; out through the mouth for 8. Three or four rounds. Stop if lightheaded.
- **Mantra meditation:** choose a short, neutral, pleasant word or sound with no strong meaning attached. Sit comfortably, eyes closed, and repeat it silently and easily; when you notice you have drifted, come back to it without effort. Start with 10 minutes, once or twice a day. Doug has practiced mantra meditation for nearly 40 years, and his experience is the most credible part of this module. If he teaches a specific tradition, say so and credit it.
- **Worry dump:** 3 minutes on paper before bed. Every open loop out of your head, each with a next action or "not this week".
- **Shutdown ritual:** a fixed end to the workday. Check tomorrow's top priority, close the loops list, say a closing phrase ("done for today"). It tells the brain the day is over.
- **Sleep settings:** the same wake time every day; outdoor light early; a caffeine cut-off in the early afternoon; a cool, dark room; phone charging outside the bedroom; a 30-minute wind-down with no work email.

None of this is medical advice. Anyone with a sleep or health condition should work with their doctor, and the program materials should say so.
