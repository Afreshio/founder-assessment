The 60-second reset before a hard call.

Inhale for 4. Hold for 7. Exhale for 8. One minute, no app, works anywhere: before a sales call, a tough conversation, or the email you are about to send. Try it right here.

How it works

4-7-8, step by step

  1. Breathe all the way outThrough your mouth, until your lungs are empty.
  2. In through your nose for 4Quietly, filling from the belly up.
  3. Hold for 7Relaxed, not straining.
  4. Out through your mouth for 8Slow and complete. The long exhale is the whole point.

Three rounds takes about a minute. The pattern was popularized by Dr. Andrew Weil. Most people find that the long, slow exhale helps them settle and think before they speak.

If you feel lightheaded, stop and breathe normally. Start with three or four rounds.
