The model

B = MAP

BJ Fogg's research at Stanford comes down to one line: a Behavior happens when Motivation, Ability and a Prompt show up at the same moment.

  • M Motivation. Most owners try to fix this one. It is the least reliable of the three.
  • A Ability. Make the behavior so small it works on your worst day.
  • P Prompt. Attach it to something you already do, so you never have to remember.

Fix ability and prompt, and motivation stops mattering so much. Then celebrate, because the feeling of success is what wires the habit in.
