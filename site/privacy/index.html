Privacy

Applies to the Afresh assessments, tools and email series on this site.

What we collect

When you take an assessment or ask for an email series, we collect your first name, your email address, your answers, and anything you choose to type in an optional field. We also record which page and ad brought you here (for example the utm tags in the link).

How we use it

To show and email your result, to send the email series you asked for, and to follow up about coaching. You can unsubscribe from any email with one click. We do not sell your information.

Cookies and advertising

These pages use the Meta Pixel to measure which ads lead to sign-ups and bookings, and may use analytics cookies for the same purpose. Meta may use this information under its own privacy policy. You can manage ad preferences in your Facebook and Instagram settings.

Who processes it

Your details are stored in our CRM (HubSpot) and our email provider. Bookings go through Calendly.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email doug@afresh.io.
