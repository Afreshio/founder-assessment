The idea

Power = Voltage² ÷ Resistance

A bulb only gets the power that makes it past the resistance in the circuit. The rest turns into heat.

  • V Your drive: energy, sleep, focus, the reason you started.
  • R What is in the way: fear, resentment, anger.
  • P What actually reaches the business: decisions made, calls taken, growth.

When results stall, most owners push more voltage: more hours, more willpower. You can only push so hard for so long. Every bit of resistance you clear lets the same effort deliver more, without working more.

The three resistors

What the heat looks like in a business

Fear

The call you have not made. The price you have not raised. The hire, or the firing, that keeps slipping to next month. From the inside, it feels like being busy.

Resentment

The partner not pulling their weight. The client who took advantage. The employee you have quietly given up on. It keeps score in the background.

Anger

The sharp email. The meeting that goes quiet. The team that stops bringing you bad news. Fast, and expensive.
